# Reading and interactive textbook

## Direction contract

THESIS: Follow a current path, then turn it into a design decision. Extend the established blue/teal technical book into a distinctive chapter atlas; preserve calm, continuous prose and native navigation.

OWN-WORLD: White reading paper, cool blue margins, ink #173c50, current teal #126e63, spectral blue #315bc5 and flicker amber #9b5b18. Chapter numbers denote the actual curriculum sequence. Serif Chinese chapter titles, system sans prose, tabular numerals only for measured data. No decorative dashboard or card grid.

STORY: Locate a chapter, read the physical question, inspect its circuit or PSD, change one frequency band and understand the variance contribution. Main text remains complete without scripts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Branded masthead with native search, recognizable book navigation, a title with generous opening space, and a readable single column. Home offers the chapter sequence and one noise exploration entry. Reading pages show position and native contents. Noise interaction pairs a spectrum with variance per frequency decade and one primary RMS result.

FORM: Read mode, code-led extension of the published book rather than a replacement identity. User delegates composition and implementation. seed: none (incumbent extension; no direction roll).

MOTION: Band edges and their integrals follow one interruptible 180 ms transition. Each frame recomputes from the displayed bounds. Source changes are immediate, never a synthetic mixture of process curves. Navigation gets a short native view transition; default content stays visible. Reduced motion updates immediately. No perpetual motion.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
